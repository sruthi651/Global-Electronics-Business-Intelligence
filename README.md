# 📌 Project Overview

This project simulates a real-world Business Intelligence solution developed for the executive leadership team of **Global Electronics**, a multinational retail company.

The objective was to transform raw transactional data into meaningful business insights through interactive Power BI dashboards, enabling executives to monitor company performance, identify trends, and make data-driven decisions.

The project follows a complete Business Intelligence workflow including:

- Data Cleaning
- SQL Data Modeling
- Relationship Creation
- Data Transformation
- KPI Development
- DAX Calculations
- Interactive Dashboard Design

---

# 🎯 Business Problem

The executive management team required a centralized reporting solution capable of answering critical business questions such as:

- How is the company performing overall?
- Which products generate the highest revenue and profit?
- Who are our most valuable customers?
- Which regions contribute the most revenue?
- Which geographical areas require business attention?
- How are stores performing across different countries?

Instead of manually analyzing thousands of sales records, this Power BI solution provides instant insights through interactive dashboards.

# 📊 Data Modeling

The Power BI model was built using a relational star schema connecting:

- Sales
- Customers
- Products
- Stores
- Exchange Rates

Relationships were established using primary and foreign keys to ensure accurate filtering and KPI calculations.

# 💡 Key Business Insights

- Computers generated the highest revenue among all product categories.
- The United States was the highest revenue-generating market.
- Customers aged **36–45** contributed the largest share of revenue.
- Large physical stores consistently outperformed smaller stores in revenue generation.
- Revenue peaked before declining in later years, indicating opportunities for further business investigation.
- Regional analysis highlighted both top-performing and underperforming markets, enabling targeted operational decisions.
